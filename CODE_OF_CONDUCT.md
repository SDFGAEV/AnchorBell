# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make AnchorBell a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming
open-source project for contributors with different backgrounds and experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and precise.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, evidence, and behavior.
- Explain safety, reproducibility, or architecture concerns clearly.
- Respect project boundaries and other contributors' ownership.
- Keep credentials, private data, and sensitive operational details out of public
  discussions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of
secrets, and knowingly misleading claims about trading performance or test
evidence are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or limit participation when necessary to
protect contributors and the integrity of the project. Report concerns privately
through the repository's security contact.
